1. The Big Mad Drip Prompt Formula
For consistent results, build prompts in layers. The strongest prompts describe the product first, then the team identity, design
language, placement, athlete or presentation style, and finally the output quality.
1
SPORT / PRODUCT
Football uniform, flag compression set, basketball jersey, bag, glove, logo, etc.
2
TEAM IDENTITY
Team name, mascot, city, number, supplied logo.
3
COLOR HIERARCHY
Primary color, secondary color, accent color; say which must dominate.
4
DESIGN LANGUAGE
Aggressive, clean, futuristic, claw marks, honeycomb, lightning, smoke, geometric, etc.
5
GARMENT DETAILS
Sleeves, hood, neckline, compression/loose fit, shorts/pants, number placement.
6
MODEL / POSE
Male/female/youth athlete, appearance, holding/kicking/throwing/serving a ball.
7
PRESENTATION
White studio background, front/back proof, isolated product, transparent PNG.
8
QUALITY LOCK
High-definition, crisp edges, realistic fabric, professional sportswear mockup.
Core rule: when revising an existing design, explicitly say what must change and what must stay unchanged. This helps preserve
the logo, face, pattern, layout, and proportions.
Universal Master Uniform Prompt
Create a premium custom [SPORT] uniform for [TEAM NAME]. Use [PRIMARY COLOR] as the dominant main color,
[SECONDARY COLOR] as the secondary color, and [ACCENT COLOR] for sharp accents. Incorporate the supplied [TEAM
LOGO / MASCOT] prominently on the uniform. Add [PATTERN / GRAPHIC LANGUAGE] throughout the design in a bold but
professional way. The uniform should include [GARMENT DETAILS: sleeve length, neckline, hood/no hood,
compression/loose fit, shorts/pants]. Place player number [NUMBER] on [PLACEMENT]. Add [CITY / TEAM WORDMARK]
where appropriate. Make the design look like premium modern custom sportswear made for competition - aggressive,
clean, athletic, and visually distinctive. Use realistic performance fabric, sublimated graphics, crisp seams,
accurate proportions, and high-definition detail. Present it on [MODEL DESCRIPTION / MANNEQUIN / FLAT PRODUCT] in
[POSE]. Use a clean white studio background. Keep the full uniform visible from head to toe when using an
athlete. No extra logos, no random text, no watermarks.
2. Logo Creation System
Create the logo before the uniform when a team does not already have a strong identity. Ask for a clean primary mark first,
then build secondary marks and wordmarks.
Aggressive Mascot Logo
Create a bold high-definition sports logo for a team named [TEAM NAME]. The mascot is a [MASCOT]. Use [PRIMARY],
[SECONDARY], and [ACCENT] as the only major colors. Make the mascot look powerful, competitive, intimidating, and
athletic without becoming overly complicated. Use sharp professional vector-style linework, strong outlines,
clean negative space, balanced symmetry, and a modern elite sports branding aesthetic. Integrate the words "[TEAM
NAME]" in a custom athletic wordmark that feels like part of the logo. The logo must reproduce clearly on
jerseys, hats, bags, gloves, social graphics, and embroidery. Center the logo on a transparent background. No
mockup, no shirt, no scenery, no watermark. High-definition isolated logo only.
Clean Modern Team Logo
Design a clean modern sports identity for [TEAM NAME] using [COLORS]. Build the mark around [MASCOT / LETTER /
SYMBOL]. Make it bold enough for a football helmet or jersey chest but simple enough to remain readable at small
sizes. Use strong geometry, professional typography, crisp vector-like edges, and a premium national-level sports
brand feel. Transparent background. Isolated logo only.

4. Home, Away & Alternate Colorways
Once the first design is approved, preserve the design language and change only the color hierarchy.
White Away Version
Redo this exact uniform as the away version. Keep the same logo, pattern, number placement, garment cut, model,
proportions, and overall design. Make WHITE the dominant main color. Use [TEAM PRIMARY] and [TEAM SECONDARY] only
as accents. Do not redesign the uniform. Do not change the athlete's face or pose unless requested. Keep every
other detail the same. High-definition, clean white studio background.
Alternate Color Version
Create an alternate colorway of this exact approved uniform. Change the dominant color from [CURRENT] to [NEW
MAIN COLOR]. Preserve the logo, pattern, typography, number placement, garment construction, and overall layout
exactly. Rebalance [SECONDARY/ACCENT COLORS] so the uniform remains readable and premium. Do not introduce new
graphics.
Back View
Show the back of this exact uniform. Keep the design, colors, fabric, athlete, and proportions consistent with
the approved front view. Add player name "[PLAYER]" across the upper back and number [NUMBER] centered below it.
Continue the approved pattern naturally onto the back and sleeves. Do not redesign anything else. White studio
background, high-definition.
5. Product Proof & Production Prompts
Uniform-Only Proof
Remove the athlete and show only the complete [SPORT] uniform as a clean product proof. Display the front and
back clearly on a white background. Preserve the exact approved colors, logo, pattern, number placement,
neckline, sleeves, shorts/pants, and proportions. No body, no mannequin face, no props, no scenery.
High-definition e-commerce/product-development presentation.
Pattern Extraction
Isolate and recreate only the graphic pattern used on this uniform. Remove the athlete, garment seams, logos,
numbers, names, and background. Show the pattern flat, clean, seamless-looking, and high-definition so it can be
referenced for sublimation artwork. Preserve the exact shapes, visual rhythm, and color relationships of the
approved design.
Transparent Logo Output
Show only the approved logo. Remove the uniform, athlete, mockup, shadows, and background. Preserve the logo
exactly. Clean the edges, improve clarity, and output it as a high-definition isolated graphic on a transparent
background. Do not redesign the logo.

Football Gloves
Create matching football gloves for [TEAM NAME] using the exact approved uniform colors and graphic pattern.

7. Athlete & Action-Shot Prompt Add-Ons
Attach one of these lines to a uniform prompt after the design description.
 Show a Black female athlete with an athletic build, confident expression, and realistic proportions.
 Show a dark-haired Hispanic/Latina female athlete with an athletic build and a strong competitive pose.
 Show a white male athlete with blond hair and an athletic build.
 Show a youth athlete with age-appropriate proportions and a confident game-ready stance.
 Have the athlete hold the football and point directly toward the camera.
 Show the athlete throwing the football with realistic throwing mechanics.
 Show the soccer player actively kicking the ball.
 Show the volleyball player serving the ball with an athletic overhead motion.
 Show the athlete flexing while holding the ball, with the full uniform still visible.
 Keep the athlete's face exactly the same as the reference image; change only the requested uniform details.
8. Revision Prompt Library
The fastest way to revise an approved design is to use short, surgical instructions.
Change main color
Keep everything exactly the same. Change only the main uniform color to [COLOR]. Preserve the logo, graphics,
pattern, number, athlete, pose, fit, and all other details.
Swap logo
Replace only the current logo with the supplied [NEW LOGO]. Match its placement and scale to the existing design.
Do not change the colors, pattern, athlete, uniform cut, or any other element.
Remove number
Remove number [NUMBER] from [LOCATION]. Keep every other part of the image exactly the same.
Continue sleeves
Continue the existing uniform pattern naturally across both sleeves so the sleeves feel fully integrated with the
body design. Do not change anything else.
Match accessories
Make the cleats, socks, gloves, and accessories coordinate with the approved uniform colors and pattern. Keep the
uniform itself unchanged.
Remove hood
Remove the hood and convert the top to a clean [CREW NECK / V-NECK]. Keep the exact same pattern, colors, logo,
fit, and athlete.
Add hood
Add a performance hood to this exact design and continue the approved pattern onto the hood. Keep everything else
unchanged.

Build a complete premium sportswear collection for [TEAM NAME] in the sport of [SPORT]. Use [PRIMARY] as the
dominant color, [SECONDARY] as the secondary color, and [ACCENT] as the accent. Use the supplied [LOGO] as the
